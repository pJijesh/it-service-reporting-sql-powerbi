## About ITIL

The notes use the vocabulary of ITIL, a framework for IT service management: incident, service request,
problem, change enablement, service level management. They paraphrase common practice and do not reproduce any
ITIL publication. The repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owner of ITIL.

ITIL is a registered trademark of PeopleCert, whose acknowledgements page names the owner as PeopleCert
International Limited and its affiliates (checked on the owner's website in November 2026). ITIL 4 is the version
the vocabulary here follows. The owner's website describes ITIL 5 as being introduced in phases and ITIL 4 as
remaining available. Nothing in these notes depends on which version an organisation has adopted.

The notes are practitioner guidance, not professional advice. Targets in the example data are illustrations for a
fictional group. Targets in a real organisation come from its contracts, its risk appetite and its budget.
